About AutoCaption AI

Understand how our AI-powered caption engine works

AI-Powered Analysis

Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, our vision engine deeply analyzes every element of your image — objects, people, colors, composition, mood, and context — to generate accurate, contextually rich captions.

Platform-Specific Captions

Each social media platform has its own culture, tone, and character limits. Our engine tailors captions specifically for LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest, and more.

Accessibility First

Every image gets optimized alt-text for screen readers, ensuring your content is accessible to all audiences. WCAG-compliant descriptions that convey essential visual information.

Privacy Focused

Your data stays yours. Use the Logout button to completely wipe all stored images, captions, and session data. Every visit starts fresh with zero residual data.
